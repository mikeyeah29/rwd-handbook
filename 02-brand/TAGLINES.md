# Taglines

Taglines should reinforce the central RWD belief:

**A great website creates momentum.**

The best lines should feel clear, confident and useful. They should point towards Website Momentum without sounding like hype.

## Pinned

- **A great website creates momentum.**
- **Don't build another website. Build an asset.**

## Strong Candidates

- **Websites built to grow.**
- **Built to evolve.**
- **Build a website with momentum.**
- **Turn your website into an asset.**
- **Websites that build momentum.**
- **Websites with more purpose after launch.**

## Direction

Good tagline territory:

- Progress
- Momentum
- Growth
- Long-term value
- Compounding value
- Websites as business assets

Avoid lines that sound too much like generic web design, motivational business language or literal rocket/space themes.

Also avoid language that implies a website is never finished just so the client keeps paying. The emphasis should be on value, usefulness and momentum, not endless work.
