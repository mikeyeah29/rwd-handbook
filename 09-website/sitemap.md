# Rockett Web Design — Sitemap

This document defines the planned structure of the Rockett Web Design website.

The website should make it easy for visitors to understand:

- What RWD does.
- Who RWD works with.
- Which service is most relevant to them.
- Why RWD is different.
- What they should do next.

The site should remain focused and avoid unnecessary pages.

---
