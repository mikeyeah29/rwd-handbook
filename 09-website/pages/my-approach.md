# How I work

A lot of WordPress sites are weighed down by page builders, bloated themes, and a mess of plugins. They look fine at first, but they’re slow, hard to maintain and almost impossible to scale. I aim to have websites be modern, maintainable and future proof.

1. Gutenberg-first - I build with the native block editor and custom code. Your sites stay fast, clean, and future-proof.
2. Lightweight & scalable - No plugin clutter. Just the functionality you need, coded properly.
3. Performance-focused - Every site I deliver is optimized for speed, SEO, and user experience from day one.
