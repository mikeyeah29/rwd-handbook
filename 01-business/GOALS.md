# Rockett Web Design — Goals

This document defines RWD's long-term business goals. These goals guide strategic decisions, product development and prioritisation across the business.

# Primary Goal

Build a sustainable WordPress business that creates long-term value for clients while generating predictable recurring revenue.

The business should become progressively less dependent on selling time and increasingly driven by systems, products and long-term partnerships.

The long-term objective is to build a business that:

Generates £6,000+ per month in sustainable revenue.
Requires no more than 4–6 hours of focused work per day, 4 days per week.
Delivers exceptional value to clients through continuous improvement rather than one-off projects.
Creates time and freedom to invest in new products, learning, creativity and family, without sacrificing business growth.

Every strategic decision should move the business closer to these outcomes.
