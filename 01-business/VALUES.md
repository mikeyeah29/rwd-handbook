# Values

## Your website should be one of your biggest growth assets.

Like your business, a website is a living asset, not a finished project.

---

## Build for the long term

Choose solutions that are maintainable and scalable.

---

## Native before plugins

Prefer WordPress core functionality before introducing unnecessary dependencies.

---

## Simplicity wins

The simplest solution is usually the best solution.

---

## AI amplifies expertise

Use AI to remove repetitive work, not replace human judgement.

---

## Solve problems, not features

Every piece of work should solve a real business problem.
