# Rockett Web Design — Business Overview

Rockett Web Design (RWD) is a WordPress development and website-care business run by Mike Rockett.

## Mission

To build WordPress websites that evolve with the businesses they support, combining expert development, AI and automation to create long-term digital assets rather than short-term projects.

## Core Services

### 1. White-Label Agency Work

RWD works behind the scenes as a development partner for digital and creative agencies.

This includes:

- Building custom WordPress and Gutenberg websites from supplied designs
- Developing custom themes, blocks, plugins and functionality
- Supporting agencies that need dependable extra development capacity
- Working under the agency's brand when required

The emphasis is on reliable delivery, clean development and becoming a trusted long-term extension of the agency's team.

### 2. Website Momentum

Website Momentum is RWD's flagship recurring service and the foundation of long-term client relationships.

Rather than simply maintaining a website, Website Momentum keeps it moving forward through continuous improvement. By combining expert WordPress development with AI and automation, every website is regularly reviewed, refined and optimised to deliver greater value over time.

Depending on the plan, this may include:

WordPress updates, backups, security and monitoring
Performance and accessibility improvements
SEO monitoring and optimisation
Analytics, reporting and actionable recommendations
Conversion and content improvements
AI-powered business and marketing automations
Proactive monthly development and feature enhancements

The goal is simple: a great website should create momentum. Every month should leave the website faster, stronger, more effective and better aligned with the client's business goals.

### 3. Custom Website Builds

RWD designs and develops bespoke WordPress websites for businesses that need something tailored to their goals, audience and brand.

The 3 main industries served / targeted are..
- Therapists / coaches
- Estate Agents
- Small Businesses

Websites should be:

- Easy for the client to manage
- Fast, secure and accessible
- Built with Gutenberg and native WordPress features where practical
- Designed to evolve rather than require frequent complete rebuilds
- Capable of moving into an ongoing Website Care plan after launch

## Business Model

RWD combines project-based development with recurring revenue.

The business is intentionally structured around three complementary service types:

1. White-Label Development provides consistent agency partnerships.
2. Custom Websites generate new client relationships and project revenue.
3. Website Momentum creates recurring revenue while continually increasing value for existing clients.

This model allows RWD to build long-term relationships rather than relying solely on acquiring new projects.

## Target Audience

RWD focuses on organisations that value quality, long-term thinking and trusted technical expertise.

Primary audiences include:

Digital and creative agencies
Estate agents
Therapists and coaches
Small businesses

These audiences value dependable partnerships, thoughtful development and websites that continue to improve over time.
