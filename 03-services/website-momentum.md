# What Is Momentum?

Momentum is the process of continually improving a website so it becomes more valuable to the business over time.

Most websites are launched with enthusiasm, then gradually become outdated. Content gets stale, opportunities are missed, performance declines, and the business evolves while the website stays the same.

Website Momentum is designed to prevent that.

Rather than treating a website as a finished project, we treat it as a living business asset. Every month we identify the highest-impact improvements, implement them, measure the results and repeat the process.

The goal isn't simply to maintain a website—it's to help it generate more value through increased visibility, better user experience, stronger conversions and smarter automation.

---

# How Do We Create Momentum?

Every improvement we make falls into one of six core areas.

## 1. Foundations

Keep the website secure, stable and running smoothly.

Without a solid foundation, meaningful improvements become difficult and risky.

### Examples

- Security monitoring
- Plugin, theme and WordPress updates
- Daily backups
- Malware scanning
- Uptime monitoring
- Bug fixes
- Broken link monitoring

**Outcome:** A secure, reliable website that's ready for continual improvement.

---

## 2. Performance

Make the website faster, more reliable and technically stronger.

### Examples

- Core Web Vitals
- Image optimisation
- Faster hosting
- Caching
- Database optimisation
- Broken link fixes
- Accessibility improvements

**Outcome:** A faster, healthier website.

---

## 3. Visibility

Help more people discover the website.

### Examples

- SEO improvements
- Keyword research
- Internal linking
- Schema markup
- Local SEO
- Landing pages
- Google Business Profile optimisation
- Technical SEO improvements

**Outcome:** More qualified visitors.

---

## 4. Conversion

Turn more visitors into enquiries and customers.

### Examples

- Better calls to action
- Simplified forms
- Improved layouts
- Copywriting improvements
- A/B testing
- Trust signals
- Testimonials
- Landing page optimisation
- User journey improvements

**Outcome:** More enquiries from the same traffic.

---

## 5. Content

Keep the website fresh, useful and relevant.

### Examples

- New service pages
- Blog articles
- Case studies
- FAQs
- Portfolio updates
- Image and media improvements
- Content refreshes
- Resource libraries

**Outcome:** A website that continually demonstrates expertise, builds trust and creates new opportunities to attract visitors.

---

## 6. Automation & Growth

Use technology to save time, improve customer experience and help the website evolve alongside the business.

### Examples

- AI assistants
- CRM integrations
- Lead qualification
- Email automations
- Appointment booking
- Workflow automation
- New functionality
- New landing pages
- New service launches
- Seasonal campaigns

**Outcome:** A website that not only supports the business today, but continues to grow with it.

# How We Work

Every business is different, so there isn't a fixed monthly checklist.

Instead, we maintain a prioritised Momentum backlog—a list of improvements that could make the biggest difference to your website.

Each month we:

1. Review performance, analytics and opportunities.
2. Prioritise the highest-impact improvements.
3. Implement those improvements.
4. Measure the results.
5. Repeat.

This means we're always working on what matters most, rather than simply completing a predefined list of maintenance tasks.

---

# Deliverables

1. An updated Momentum Backlog, prioritised with recommendations for what to tackle next.
2. A report showing what was done, what could be improved and any measureable improvements.
