# Managed Hosting and Maintenance

Last updated: 23 September 2026

## Product summary

Managed Hosting and Maintenance is a simple recurring service for clients who want their RWD-built WordPress website kept online, backed up, monitored and technically maintained without paying for continuous improvement work.

It is a productised supporting service rather than a fourth core service. It gives suitable website clients an affordable aftercare route while preserving Website Momentum as the higher-value service for businesses that need their website to become more effective over time.

> Reliable hosting and essential WordPress maintenance for clients who want their website looked after after launch.

## Strategic role

The offer has three purposes:

- Give clients with simple needs a proportionate alternative to Website Momentum.
- Protect RWD-built websites from avoidable neglect after launch.
- Maintain a long-term client relationship without implying that every client needs monthly improvement work.

It should not be presented as a replacement for Website Momentum. Managed Hosting and Maintenance protects the website's technical baseline; Momentum uses that baseline to create additional business value.

## Best-fit customer

The service is intended for a client who:

- Has a straightforward WordPress website built by RWD.
- Wants someone else to handle hosting, backups, updates and monitoring.
- Does not currently need regular SEO, content, conversion or development work.
- Accepts that changes and improvements are quoted separately.
- Does not require an ecommerce, membership or other operationally critical support agreement.

Initially, the service should only be offered for websites built by RWD on a known, supportable stack. Do not accept responsibility for an unfamiliar WordPress website at this price. If the offer is later opened to existing third-party websites, require a paid technical assessment and any necessary remediation before acceptance.

## Recommended price

- **£30 per month** on a rolling monthly agreement.
- **£300 per year** when paid annually in advance.

Confirm whether public prices include or exclude VAT before publishing them.

The price is viable because RWD controls the build quality and can standardise and automate much of the routine work. It must not become an open-ended support retainer.

## Included

- Managed WordPress hosting for one eligible website.
- SSL certificate setup and renewal.
- Daily automated backups with a suitable retention period.
- WordPress core, theme and plugin updates for the supported stack.
- Uptime and basic security monitoring.
- Recovery from a suitable backup when the managed hosting environment or an update performed under the plan causes a fault.
- Notification when a detected issue requires client action or separately quoted work.
- Reasonable email communication about services covered by the plan.

The final service agreement should state the update schedule, backup retention period, monitoring tools and normal response expectations once the delivery stack has been selected.

## Not included

- Content edits or uploads.
- New pages, features, integrations or design changes.
- SEO, analytics, reporting or conversion work.
- Proactive performance optimisation beyond maintaining the agreed hosting configuration.
- Domain registration, business email or third-party platform fees unless stated separately.
- Faults caused by client changes, unsupported software or third-party services.
- Malware removal, emergency development or data recovery when a clean supported backup is unavailable.
- Meetings, consultancy, an uptime guarantee or an unlimited support allowance.

Work outside the plan can be quoted separately. If a client regularly requests improvements, recommend Website Momentum instead of repeatedly stretching the hosting plan.

## Client responsibilities

The client remains responsible for:

- Paying for and retaining control of their domain name.
- Keeping contact and billing details current.
- Using secure account credentials and protecting their own devices and accounts.
- Maintaining subscriptions and access for any third-party services used by the website.
- Avoiding unapproved plugins, themes or technical changes that make the supported stack unsafe or difficult to maintain.
- Responding when RWD identifies a decision, renewal or separately chargeable issue.

## Relationship to Website Momentum

Managed Hosting and Maintenance and Website Momentum serve different needs.

| Managed Hosting and Maintenance | Website Momentum |
| --- | --- |
| Keeps the website hosted, backed up, monitored and maintained. | Includes the same technical foundation and proactively improves the website. |
| Reactive within a tightly defined technical scope. | Proactive, prioritised and commercially focused. |
| No recurring content, SEO, conversion or development work. | Can include performance, SEO, content, conversion, automation and development. |
| Fixed low-cost product for straightforward RWD-built sites. | Higher-value ongoing partnership for websites that materially affect business performance. |

A client should use one route, not purchase both. The technical care provided by this product is included within Website Momentum.

## Post-launch choices

Every suitable website client should receive three clear choices:

1. **Client-managed handover** — the client arranges hosting and becomes responsible for maintenance.
2. **Managed Hosting and Maintenance** — RWD handles the defined technical essentials.
3. **Website Momentum** — RWD handles the technical essentials and ongoing improvement.

Ongoing support is optional. The proposal must make ownership, third-party costs and transfer arrangements clear whichever route the client selects.

## Cancellation and transfer

The client continues to own their domain, content and completed website. Cancelling the plan must not trap them on RWD infrastructure.

When the service ends:

- The client must arrange replacement hosting and ongoing maintenance before the paid service period expires.
- RWD should provide a clean handover or suitable recent backup within the agreed process.
- Straightforward handover information is included; substantial migration, troubleshooting or reconfiguration can be quoted separately.
- Hosting, monitoring and maintenance end when the paid service period expires or an agreed migration completes.

The final service agreement should define notice periods, data retention after cancellation and what happens when payment fails.

## Operational dependency

Do not create a detailed delivery playbook or publish operational promises until RWD has selected:

- The hosting platform and account structure.
- Backup tooling, storage location and retention policy.
- Update and rollback workflow.
- Uptime and security monitoring tools.
- Supported plugin and theme policy.
- Incident triage and client communication process.

Once these decisions are made, create `10-playbooks/managed-hosting-maintenance.md` as the internal delivery procedure. This product document should remain the commercial source of truth.
