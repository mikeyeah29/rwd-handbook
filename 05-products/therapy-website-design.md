# Therapy Website Design

Last updated: 22 September 2026

## Product summary

Therapy Website Design is a productised website service for UK counsellors, psychotherapists and practitioner psychologists in solo private practice.

It combines a proven WordPress foundation with enough strategy, content guidance and visual tailoring to make the finished website feel specific to the practitioner. The client is not buying a generic template with their details inserted. They are buying a guided route to a clear, credible and useful website without paying for an entirely bespoke design and development process.

The standard offer is deliberately narrow:

> A professionally planned, personally tailored five-page website that helps suitable clients find the practice, understand the practitioner and take the next step.

Larger practices, unusual functionality and projects requiring original art direction move into a separate bespoke service.

## Positioning

### Customer-facing description

> A calm, professional website built around your practice—not around a generic therapist template. I will help you organise what you need to say, tailor the design to reflect you and take care of the WordPress build, technical setup and launch.

### What is being productised

The repeatable parts of the work are:

- The discovery questionnaire and planning process
- The recommended information architecture
- The Enigma WordPress design system and component library
- The core page types and conversion journey
- The technical, SEO, performance and accessibility checklist
- The review, launch and handover process

The parts that remain personal to each client are:

- Their audience, specialisms and services
- The words, credentials and professional claims used on the site
- Their photography and other practice assets
- The colour, typography and visual treatment
- The order and emphasis of page content
- The way a prospective client enquires or books

This makes the work efficient without making the result impersonal.

## Best-fit customer

The standard package is best suited to a practitioner who:

- Runs, or is preparing to start, a solo private practice
- Is a counsellor, psychotherapist or practitioner psychologist
- Offers a focused set of related services
- Needs a new website or a straightforward redesign
- Wants guidance with structure and sections but can supply the finished words for each page
- Has an existing logo or is comfortable with a simple typographic identity
- Uses a standard enquiry or third-party booking process
- Values professional support but cannot justify a large bespoke agency budget

It is also suitable for a small two-person practice when both practitioners share the same brand, services and enquiry process. The second practitioner profile counts as an additional standard page. More complex group practices move to the bespoke route.

## Problems the product must solve

The product should preserve the value this audience needs, even though the delivery is streamlined.

### 1. The practice is difficult to find or relies too heavily on directories

The website provides a technically sound, search-friendly home for the practice. It includes clear page topics, location and service information, page titles, meta descriptions, structured headings, internal links, image alt text, schema where appropriate, an XML sitemap and Google Search Console setup.

This is a foundation for visibility, not a promise of immediate rankings. Ongoing SEO, content creation and local search work are separate services.

### 2. The practitioner struggles to explain who they help

The discovery and content process helps the practitioner move from broad lists of modalities and issues towards clear explanations of:

- Who the practice is for
- What those clients may be experiencing
- How the practitioner works
- What makes the approach or setting relevant
- What a prospective client should do next

The standard package includes the page structure, recommended sections and prompts that tell the client what each section needs to communicate. The client supplies the finished words. RWD places and formats that content within the design but does not provide copywriting or substantial rewriting.

### 3. The current website does not reflect the quality of the work

The design is tailored using the client's real practice, personality and preferences. Colour, typography, imagery, content hierarchy and selected layouts are adapted within the Enigma design system so that the result feels credible and individual without requiring a ground-up design system.

### 4. The website task consumes too much time and headspace

The client follows one guided process, receives clear decisions and deadlines, and does not need to coordinate a designer, developer, SEO specialist and content strategist separately. RWD owns the delivery from initial plan through to launch and handover.

### Included scope

#### Planning and content direction

- One structured discovery questionnaire
- One 60-minute planning call
- Definition of the primary audience, services and enquiry route
- A recommended sitemap and page plan
- Page-by-page section plans and content prompts
- Placement and formatting of client-supplied copy within the agreed design
- Correction of obvious typographical errors during the build
- Advice on photography, credentials, testimonials and calls to action

This is content structure, not copywriting. The client remains responsible for the accuracy, tone and final wording of every page.

#### Design

- One tailored visual direction using the Enigma design system
- Client-specific colour and typography settings
- Thoughtful use of the supplied logo, photography and brand assets
- Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop
- One consolidated design review round

This does not include a new brand identity, custom illustration system or several competing design concepts. If the client has no logo, a simple typographic name-mark can be added to the project; this is defined under Optional additions.

#### Five core pages

The exact sitemap is agreed during planning. A typical site includes:

1. Home
2. About
3. How I Help or Services
4. Fees and FAQs
5. Contact

The core pages can be rearranged to suit the practice. For example, a focused service page may replace a separate FAQs page.

Privacy, cookie and terms pages can use a standard text-page layout without counting towards the five core pages, but the client must supply or obtain the legal wording. RWD does not provide legal advice.

#### WordPress build and technical setup

- WordPress setup using the Enigma theme
- Navigation, header and footer
- Standard contact or enquiry form
- A standard link to an existing third-party booking system
- Social and professional-directory links where relevant
- Basic analytics and consent setup
- Foundational on-page SEO
- Relevant basic schema
- Image compression, caching and performance checks
- Responsive, accessibility and cross-browser checks
- Basic security, backup and anti-spam configuration
- Basic redirects for up to five existing page URLs when replacing a current website
- One consolidated review round on the completed website

#### Launch and handover

- Deployment to the agreed hosting environment
- Domain and SSL connection
- Final form, link and device checks
- XML sitemap submission to Google Search Console
- One recorded handover or a 45-minute live training session
- Administrator access and ownership handover
- 30 days of post-launch support for faults within the agreed scope

### Client responsibilities

The fixed price depends on the client:

- Completing the questionnaire and giving one set of consolidated feedback
- Supplying accurate credentials, fees, contact details and service information
- Supplying final approved copy for every page before the build begins
- Supplying a usable logo or selecting the simple name-mark addition
- Supplying usable photography or approving recommended stock imagery
- Supplying privacy, cookie and other legal wording
- Maintaining access to their domain, email, booking platform and relevant accounts
- Responding within the agreed project schedule

Delays or changes caused by missing content should move the launch date rather than create rushed or unpaid work.

### Reviews and revisions

The standard package includes two structured review points:

1. Visual direction and homepage review
2. Completed website review

Each review includes one consolidated set of reasonable amendments within the agreed scope. Additional pages use the published page price. A new visual direction is outside the package, and any replacement copy must be supplied by the client. Other changes requested after approval require the project to be re-scoped.

### Indicative timeline

Allow **three to four weeks** from the point at which the questionnaire, core content and required access are received.

The delivery process remains:

1. **Understand and plan** — clarify the practice, audience, services, content and enquiry journey.
2. **Design around the practice** — establish a visual direction and arrange the supplied content so it feels credible and personal.
3. **Build in WordPress** — build the agreed pages, integrations and technical foundations using Enigma.
4. **Prepare and launch** — test, refine, connect the domain, launch and show the client how to manage the site.

Productising the foundation does not remove this process. It removes unnecessary reinvention inside each stage.

## Bespoke route

### Bespoke Therapy Practice Website

**From £3,500**, with most projects expected to fall between **£3,500 and £6,000+** depending on scope.

A project should move to the bespoke route when it includes one or more of the following:

- A group practice with several practitioners or complex matching
- Several locations, languages or enquiry routes
- A large existing site or SEO-sensitive content migration
- Several distinct service funnels or resource libraries
- Memberships, courses, payments or gated content
- Custom booking, CRM, assessment or practice-management integrations
- Bespoke functionality, plugins or data handling
- A genuinely original visual system outside the Enigma foundation

The bespoke route can follow the same four-stage process, but discovery, design, development, reviews and pricing are scoped individually. Bespoke website pricing does not add copywriting or brand-identity services to RWD's offer; those inputs must still be supplied by the client or an appropriate specialist.

## Ongoing costs and support

Be explicit about costs after launch.

- Domain registration: paid by the client to their registrar
- Premium software or third-party platforms: agreed in advance and normally paid by the client
- Hosting, updates, backups and monitoring: optional Managed Hosting and Maintenance at **£30 per month** or **£300 per year**
- Routine content changes, SEO, new pages and improvement work: offered separately through Website Momentum or quoted work

Managed Hosting and Maintenance covers reliable hosting, SSL, WordPress and plugin updates, daily backups, uptime/basic security monitoring and recovery when the managed hosting environment or an update performed under the plan causes a fault. It does not include an open-ended allowance for content edits, new features, SEO, optimisation or general troubleshooting.

Clients who do not take Managed Hosting and Maintenance can receive a clean handover to suitable hosting, but must understand that maintenance becomes their responsibility. Clients who need proactive, prioritised improvement can choose Website Momentum, which includes the same technical foundation.

## Commercial boundaries

The product remains viable only if RWD protects the repeatable scope.

- Do not offer unlimited revisions.
- Do not include full copywriting under “content guidance.”
- Do not promise SEO results from foundational setup alone.
- Do not absorb complex migrations or integrations into the base price.
- Do not discount because the site is built on a proven system; the system is what makes the price and reliability possible.
- Do not start the build without the agreed content, access and initial payment.
- If the project no longer fits the package, pause and re-scope it rather than quietly expanding the work.

## Pricing rationale

Current UK offers cover several different service models:

- Low-cost managed template services commonly advertise roughly £16–£50 per month, often with setup fees, limited layouts or minimum terms. These are useful alternatives for highly price-sensitive clients but do not offer the same strategy, tailoring or ownership model. - Entry-level freelancer or specialist packages can begin below £1,000. Examples include a four-page therapist website at £895 and counsellor website design advertised from £750. [First Meeting](https://firstmeeting.co.uk/services/) [Berlew](https://berlew.co.uk/sector/website-design-for-counsellors/)
- Published UK guides place many professionally delivered private-practice websites in the region of £1,000–£2,500, with bespoke work around £2,000–£5,000 or more. [Psyche Creative](https://www.psychecreative.co.uk/blog/how-much-does-a-therapist-website-cost-uk) [Elite Digital Agency](https://elitedigitalagency.io/guides/private-practice-website-cost-uk/)

At £1,750, the standard package occupies a credible middle position. It is not trying to beat subscription templates on price. It is giving a solo practitioner personal strategic help and a professionally delivered site without asking them to fund a fully bespoke agency process.

Review the package price every six months, and after every three to five completed projects. Track actual hours across discovery, content, design, build, project communication, revisions, launch and support. If the standard project regularly exceeds roughly four to five focused working days, either tighten the scope or raise the price.

## Success criteria

The product is working when:

- Prospective clients understand what is included without a long sales explanation.
- Most suitable projects can be quoted from the standard package and a small number of add-ons.
- The client feels individually represented rather than placed into a generic theme.
- The site answers the practical questions a prospective therapy client needs answered.
- RWD can complete the work consistently without unpaid scope expansion.
- The client can own and update the website after handover.
- Larger projects are identified early and moved confidently into the bespoke route.
